# BOM notes

Prices are indicative concept estimates (TRL 2) and will be confirmed with named suppliers at TRL 3. Line numbers match the callouts in `media/exploded.png`; line 12 (sealant, fasteners and tubing) is not modeled.

Parts total about $241 for one 1 m2 prototype, within the $250 concept budget in `project.yaml` but with only about $9 of margin and nothing for spares or tools. The largest cost is the five aluminum plates (lines 3 and 5, about $88). Choosing 316 stainless steel condenser plates instead of coated aluminum would add roughly $100 to $150 and exceed the budget; that option is proposed, awaiting Amish, and the budget is not changed here.

Every part that touches distillate (lines 5, 8 and 12) must be food-contact rated. Spacers (line 6) must be printed in ASA or polycarbonate, not PETG, because stage 1 runs at about 70 to 80 °C and dry stagnation can exceed 100 °C.
